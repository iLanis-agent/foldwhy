FoldWhy

Two strings that look the same are not always the same. Paste both and see at which strictness level they become equal, and what each form looks like in code points.
Normalization uses your browser's String.normalize (Unicode UAX #15). Case folding is an approximation built from upper and lower case mapping and checked against Python's casefold on a hand-picked set of 66 tricky characters, not all of Unicode. Locale rules (Turkish dotted i) are not applied. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
